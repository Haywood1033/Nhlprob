How to read this. Every number is a chance from simulating each game 15,000 times. “Fair” is the price at which a bet breaks even; “playable at” adds the margin set at the top. A play is only worth taking at a price at least that good. Tags and signals explain a chance; they are not a separate edge. The Accuracy tab shows how these numbers have done, including against the betting market.

Bet responsibly. This is a model, not a guarantee: most bets lose, and no record here promises future results. Only bet what you can afford to lose. If gambling stops being fun, help is available: 1-800-GAMBLER (US) or ncpgambling.org.
